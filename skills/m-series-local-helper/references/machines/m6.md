# M6

Checked 2026-10-22. The verified variant is **base M6**. M6 Pro, Max or Ultra are not verified by this catalog; do not infer them from previous generations.

[Apple introduced M6 on August 25, 2026](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/). The [M6 Mac mini specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/128108) list a 12-core CPU/GPU and 16/24/32 GB of unified memory. The same page also contains a separate M5 Pro model: do not assign its 48/64-GB options to M6.

For this product, begin with the 4B MLX candidate at 16/24 GB or the 9B candidate at 32 GB. These are conservative initial candidates and have not been measured across all M6 configurations. A newer chip does not make a 37.7-GB 8-bit model fit comfortably into a 16-GB machine.

Prefer a supported oMLX/MLX release, but verify actual M6 initialization and execution. The reviewed oMLX README's explicit chip list stopped at M5; Apple Silicon compatibility language alone is not a successful M6 round trip. Official Splash states M3-or-newer support, but its model-specific minimum memory and OS requirements remain decisive.

Only change the candidate after correctness, tool use, task latency, memory pressure and responsiveness evidence supports it. Treat claimed M6-specific gains as unverified until measured with the exact runtime/model. See [upstream sources](../upstream-sources.md).
